Frequently Asked Questions

To book a service, simply register as a customer, login to your account, and use the "Book Service" feature. Select your preferred services, date, and time, and submit your request. An admin will assign a workshop to your booking.

Workshop registration requires admin approval. Register with your workshop details, and an admin will review and approve your account. Once approved, you can login and start managing bookings.

Login to your customer dashboard and navigate to "My Bookings" to see the current status of all your bookings. You'll receive notifications when the status changes.

Contact the assigned workshop directly or use the contact form to request changes. Workshops can update booking status through their dashboard.
