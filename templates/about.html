About AutoCare

Your complete automotive service management solution

Our Mission

To revolutionize the automotive service industry by providing a comprehensive, user-friendly platform that connects customers with trusted workshops while empowering businesses with efficient management tools.

Our Vision

To become the leading automotive service platform that ensures transparency, quality, and convenience for all stakeholders in the automotive ecosystem.

System Features

Multi-Role Support

Dedicated interfaces for customers, workshops, and administrators with role-specific features and access controls.

Smart Booking System

Advanced booking management with real-time availability, automated scheduling, and instant confirmations.

Analytics Dashboard

Comprehensive analytics and reporting tools for business insights and performance tracking.

Location Services

Google Maps integration for finding nearby workshops, getting directions, and location-based services.

Real-time Notifications

Instant notifications for booking updates, status changes, and important announcements.

Rating & Reviews

Customer feedback system with ratings, reviews, and quality assurance mechanisms.
